## Technical baseline extracted from the reference design

The provided architecture proposes a five-layer platform: API/client, orchestration and decisioning, liquidity connectivity, settlement and treasury, and compliance and risk. It assigns Go to I/O-bound payment, routing, provider-connectivity, workflow, and read-projection responsibilities; Rust to latency- and integrity-sensitive custody, ledger, and risk paths; Python to reporting, analytics, and detection work; and TypeScript to the unified interface and control-plane contracts. This assignment is treated as binding because it matches the requester’s express language constraint.

The document’s stated design assumptions include externally managed money-bearing execution, idempotent provider interactions, a mandatory policy gate before monetary execution, immutable audit evidence, and clear distinction between pending and final settlement. These controls will be implemented as enforced application invariants, not as display-only requirements.

## Unvalidated or activation-gated items

No claim in the supplied documents establishes that a particular provider is currently connected to, licensed for, contracted with, or technically available to this project. No regulatory submission endpoint, payment-rail credential, pricing feed credential, identity-verification provider, chain-analytics provider, notification provider, or regulated entity information has been supplied. Accordingly, the implementation may create verified integration interfaces and operational readiness checks, but cannot initiate funds, screen live counterparties through proprietary services, ingest protected provider feeds, or submit reports to a regulator until the requisite legal and technical authority is provided.
