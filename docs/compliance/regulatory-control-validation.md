# Regulatory Control Validation Record

## Purpose and limitations

This record translates reviewed primary-source materials into product-control requirements as of **17 August 2026**. It is an engineering control baseline, not legal advice, a licensing determination, or authority to operate. A regulated entity and qualified counsel in Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa must approve all activation decisions. The platform must not claim CBN, CBK, SARB, SEC, CMA, FSCA, FIC, OFAC, UN, or EU compliance merely because a record exists in this control plane.

## Claims removed from the activation baseline

The supplied regulatory memo includes statements whose operative legal details were not confirmed by the reviewed primary materials. The implementation therefore does **not** encode them as facts: specific permissions or restrictions under Nigeria’s 2023 VASP bank-account guidelines; a final or effective Kenya VASP Regulations 2026 regime; automatic authorisation of all South African crypto-payment activity; universal regulator submission endpoints; and any named provider’s current licence, contract, technical availability, or rail capability.

## Universal enforcement rules

All money-bearing and regulated operations require a corridor policy decision before execution. The decision requires a corridor code, product classification, acting regulated entity, verified provider authorisation, customer and counterparty eligibility, sanctions-screen result, KYC/KYB state, Travel Rule state where applicable, risk decision, and reportability assessment. Each decision must record the exact policy version, evidence references, actor, timestamp, and outcome. A missing, stale, revoked, or ambiguous prerequisite produces a blocked or manual-review outcome rather than an execution request.
