01 · Regulatory Context
What the CBN Sandbox is designed to achieve
CBN Cohort 2 priorities
Innovate responsiblyControlled testing under CBN oversight, with boundaries around users, volumes, exposure, and duration.
Promote inclusionSupport digital-finance solutions that expand access while protecting consumers.
Secure the ecosystemStrengthen integrity, resilience, transparency, and trust in payments and financial services.
Generate evidenceUse live-test evidence to inform future supervision, policy, and licensing decisions.
What this means for a VASP participant
VASP Track
Payment, settlement, wallet, custody-adjacent, fiat on/off-ramp, stablecoin, and related market-infrastructure propositions can be tested within approved parameters.
Safeguards
AML/CFT/CPF, consumer protection, cybersecurity, privacy, operational resilience, incident response, and orderly wind-down must be demonstrated.
UmojaFlowOS fit
Provide the control, evidence, and enforcement fabric around the test—not an assertion that the platform itself is a licensed bank, VASP, custodian, or settlement network.
Boundary
Sandbox admission is not a permanent licence or authorisation outside the approved testing perimeter.
