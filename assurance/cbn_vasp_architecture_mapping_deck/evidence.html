Immutable Binding

Every artifact is hashed (SHA-256) and bound to the release manifest. Any change to a file invalidates the entire evidence set.

WORM Preservation

Evidence is stored in Write-Once-Read-Many storage, preventing deletion or modification by any platform user.

Independent E-09

A final review by a subject not involved in the test execution is mandatory before a release is marked GO.
