03 · Platform Capabilities
What the platform does

Control Plane

The "brain" of the system that manages user authority, session security, and coordinates all stakeholder workflows.

Payment Engine

Validates every transaction against strict rules and ensures value only moves when all conditions are met.

Ledger Gateway

Maintains the "financial truth" by recording every event in a high-speed, permanent, and balanced double-entry ledger.

Risk & Compliance

Automated screening for AML/CFT, sanctions, and Travel Rule data to block illicit activity in real-time.

Reporting & Analytics

Transforms raw data into usable insights for regulators, providing a clear view of market impact and trends.

Document Intel

Verifies the authenticity of identity documents and detects potential deepfakes or fraudulent submissions.

Retention Gateway

Enforces data privacy by ensuring records are kept only as long as required and deleted securely when authorised.

Operations Hub

Monitors system health, manages incidents, and provides the tools for controlled rollback and recovery.
