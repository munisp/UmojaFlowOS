10 · Governance Framework

Trust by Design

Accountability

Segregation of Duties

Mandatory 12 distinct identities for primary and alternate governance roles. No single person can initiate, approve, and reconcile a transaction.

Security

Fail-Closed Controls

Automated gates for identity, AML screening, and limits. If a required control or external dependency is unavailable, the system stops automatically.

Integrity

Immutable Evidence

Material actions are recorded in WORM (Write-Once-Read-Many) storage with SHA-256 cryptographic binding, ensuring proof is permanent and tamper-proof.

Authority

Independent Approvals

Production releases require four independent sign-offs (Release, Security, Compliance, Operations) bound to the specific, verified code version.
