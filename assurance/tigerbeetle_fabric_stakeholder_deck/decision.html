TigerBeetle
Authoritative accounting
Maintains the double-entry transfer fact. It is the source for debit/credit integrity, balance movement, and settlement reconciliation.
Hyperledger Fabric
Consortium attestation
Maintains an endorsed, independently queryable proof that a release or evidence artifact was observed by approved organizations.
Boundary: Fabric can prove an event. It cannot create, settle, reverse, or authorize customer funds movement.
