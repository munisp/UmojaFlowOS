Consortium Governance
Trust is built on shared rules,
not shared secrets.
Membership & MSP
Each organization owns its Membership Service Provider (MSP) and Certificate Authority. No single party can unilaterally add members or change channel rules.
Identity & HSM
Signing keys are retained in Hardware Security Modules (HSM). Private keys never leave the organization; only endorsed digests are recorded on the ledger.
Endorsement Policy
Attestations require multi-party endorsement. A proof is only valid if the required organizations observe and sign the same evidence digest.
Change Control
Chaincode upgrades and policy changes require two-party approval from each organization, a reproducible build, and a release-manifest binding.
Independent Assurance: Quarterly reviews of membership, certificate inventory, incident records, and reconciliation results.
