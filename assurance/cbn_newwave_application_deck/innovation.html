02 · Proposed innovation
UmojaFlowOS: a controlled assurance layer
A non-custodial platform for bounded VASP-related testing—not unrestricted public financial activity.
Applicant-supplied proposal. The final legal perimeter, activities, counterparties, and regulatory classification require Newwave and Nigerian counsel approval.
01Product-boundary controls
Allowlisted capabilities; explicit exclusions for custody, issuance, and unrestricted exchange.
02Risk and compliance gates
AML/CFT/CPF, sanctions, Travel Rule, customer and transaction controls.
03Authority and reconciliation
Identity, dual control, segregation of duties, ledger posting, and reconciliation.
04Resilience and evidence
Fail-closed workflows, incident response, rollback, WORM evidence, and review.
Status: proposed concept · Not evidence of an existing Nigerian VASP operation
