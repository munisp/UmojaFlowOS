07 · Governance and Accountability
Strict Dual-Control Framework
Mandatory Accountable Officer Roles
Compliance / MLRO
Independent reporting to Board; AML/CFT/CPF oversight and STR/SAR filing authority.
Security Owner
Cybersecurity posture, mTLS/OIDC enforcement, and incident response leadership.
Product Risk
Test boundary enforcement, Naira-equivalent limit monitoring, and risk assessment.
Treasury / Custody
Asset reconciliation, payment release authority, and liquidity management.
Consumer Protection
Participant consent, complaint resolution, and wind-down/refund management.
Incident Commander
CBN liaison, material incident declaration, and recovery orchestration.

Segregation of Duties

Mandatory distinct subjects for all 6 primary and 6 alternate roles (12 unique identities). Prohibited self-approval for all material actions.

Dual-Control Approvals

Release manifest requires 4 independent sign-offs (Release, Security, Compliance, Operations) bound to the specific commit SHA.

Requirement: All roles must be evidenced by appointment letters, CVs, and fit-and-proper declarations.
