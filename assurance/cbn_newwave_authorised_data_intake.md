# Newwave CBN Sandbox application — authorised data intake

**Purpose:** complete the CBN VASP application using records supplied by an authorised Newwave representative. Do not place BVN, identity documents, bank statements, private keys, or unredacted customer data in Git.

## Corporate and regulatory identity

Provide the exact applying legal entity, certificate of incorporation, registry number, incorporation date, jurisdiction, registered address, principal business address, official monitored email, main telephone, TIN if applicable, and CEO/MD BVN only through the official secure CBN channel if required. Confirm whether the applying entity is NewWave Telecom and Technologies, Inc., NewWave Holdings, LLC, a Nigerian subsidiary, or another group company.

Confirm the Nigerian legal connection, Nigerian operating entity or partner, Nigerian office if any, local representative, and the legal analysis explaining why the proposed service belongs in the CBN VASP Track and how it interacts with SEC or other Nigerian jurisdiction.

## Ownership, UBO, and governance

Provide a signed group ownership chart; all shareholders holding at least 5%; all UBOs; UBO identification; nationality, residence, date of birth, ownership/control basis and effective date; source of wealth and source of funds; other-company positions; PEP declarations; board list; senior-management list; organogram; appointment letters; CVs; fit-and-proper declarations; and board approval.

Complete the six accountable governance roles in the UmojaFlowOS governance package with distinct primary and alternate enterprise-directory subjects:

| Role | Primary subject | Alternate subject | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compliance / MLRO | Supply real subject | Supply different real subject | Appointment, AML/CFT experience, CV, fit-and-proper, conflict, recusal, independent reporting line |
| Security | Supply real subject | Supply different real subject | Appointment, security qualifications, CV, fit-and-proper, conflict, recusal |
| Product Risk | Supply real subject | Supply different real subject | Appointment, risk qualifications, CV, fit-and-proper, conflict, recusal |
| Treasury / Custody | Supply real subject | Supply different real subject | Appointment, treasury/custody qualifications, CV, fit-and-proper, conflict, recusal |
| Consumer Protection | Supply real subject | Supply different real subject | Appointment, consumer-protection experience, CV, fit-and-proper, conflict, recusal |
| Incident Commander / CBN Liaison | Supply real subject | Supply different real subject | Appointment, incident/regulatory liaison experience, CV, fit-and-proper, conflict, recusal |

All twelve subjects must be distinct. Document owners and reviewers must also be segregated wherever the schema requires it.

## Financial and licensing evidence

Provide existing licences and registrations, regulators, licence numbers, jurisdiction, issue/expiry dates, current status, prior CBN Sandbox applications and outcomes, all enforcement/sanctions/investigation disclosures, authorised and paid-up capital, liquid assets, monthly operating expense, audited financial statements for the last three years or management accounts, audit firm, current-year revenue and cost projections, cash runway, three-month bank statements, and customer-asset coverage position if applicable.

## Product and test design

Confirm the precise VASP activity categories: exchange/trading, P2P marketplace, derivatives, third-party digital-asset custody, wallet provision, tokenised assets/RWA, broker/dealer, intermediary, payment gateway, lending/borrowing, DeFi, analytics/RegTech, NFT marketplace, or other. State what UmojaFlowOS itself does and does not do.

Provide the test duration, Nigerian geography, participant profile, volunteer-consent process, maximum single transaction value, maximum daily transaction volume, maximum monthly active users, cash/customer-asset limits, counterparties, partner institutions, success KPIs, failure thresholds, complaints process, incident-notification SLA, and signed wind-down/exit plan.

## AML/CFT/CPF and Travel Rule

Provide the AML/CFT/CPF policy; MLRO/CCO appointment; Board/Risk/Audit reporting line; customer-risk methodology; KYC tiers; EDD triggers; PEP and sanctions treatment; source-of-funds thresholds; transaction-monitoring scenarios; STR/SAR escalation and target filing time; STR count in the last 12 months; record-retention policy; Travel Rule procedure/provider; originator/beneficiary data retention; cross-border risk controls; smart-contract/DeFi controls; and independent compliance testing.

## Technology, cyber, custody, and third parties

Provide current architecture and data-flow diagrams; environment inventory; API and identity controls; encryption and key management; mTLS/OIDC/RBAC design; penetration-test date and report; vulnerability register and remediation; business continuity; disaster recovery; RTO/RPO; incident response; backup/restore tests; WORM evidence-store controls; wallet/custody percentages and multi-signature scheme if relevant; KMS/HSM provider; third-party custodian details; AML and Travel Rule providers; cloud/hosting; banks, switches, NIBSS/NRS/custodians, identity providers, and all third-party agreements and due-diligence assessments.

## Required sign-off

Before submission, obtain written approvals from Legal, Compliance/MLRO, Security, Product/Risk, Operations, Finance, and the authorised signatory. Then complete real staging E-01 through E-09 evidence collection, independent E-09 review, four release approvals, and the final no-fabricated-evidence check.
