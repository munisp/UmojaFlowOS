# 2. Board briefing

## Executive decision requested

The Board is asked to acknowledge that the application is currently **DRAFT / NO-GO**, approve the remediation programme, appoint accountable owners, authorise Nigerian counsel to determine the regulatory perimeter, and require a further Board or delegated-committee approval before any application is signed or submitted.

The Board should not approve a filing based only on public website information or technical readiness. The public NewWave pages support a preliminary company and capability narrative, but they do not establish the exact applying entity, Nigerian connection, UBOs, financial capacity, compliance appointments, or regulated activity perimeter.[1][2][3]

## Current risk position

The most material risk is **entity and ownership uncertainty**. NewWave’s public materials refer to NewWave Telecom and Technologies, Inc. and NewWave Holdings, LLC, but the application must identify the exact legal applicant and trace every direct and indirect ownership and control path to natural-person UBOs. Public titles, including Founder and Chief Executive Officer, must not be treated as proof of ownership, control, board authority, or authorised-signatory status.

A second material risk is **regulatory perimeter uncertainty**. UmojaFlowOS is proposed as a non-custodial control and evidence layer for bounded VASP-related testing. That description must be tested against the actual services, customer model, counterparties, money flows, custody arrangements, wallet operation, exchange or brokerage functions, token issuance, and Nigerian data flows. Counsel must determine whether CBN, SEC, or another authority has jurisdiction over any component.

A third material risk is **evidence incompleteness**. The DEC form requests UBO identification, source of wealth and source of funds, PEP declarations, financial records, licences, compliance officers, test limits, technical safeguards, and supporting documents. None of those should be replaced by estimates, public biographies, synthetic fixtures, or unverified assertions.

## UBO blockers requiring Board attention

| Blocker | Board-level question | Required decision or evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Applicant entity | Which legal entity will apply, contract, operate, receive funds, and bear responsibility? | Approve one entity and evidence its Nigerian connection. |
| Ownership chain | Does the chart trace all corporate owners and control rights to natural persons? | Approve signed chart, cap table, registry evidence, and legal review. |
| Non-equity control | Are there voting agreements, nominee arrangements, trusts, veto rights, or appointment rights? | Require a control-rights review and counsel conclusion. |
| UBO screening | Have all UBOs and close associates been screened for PEP, sanctions, adverse media, enforcement, and criminal matters? | Require dated results, disposition, EDD, and escalation records. |
| Wealth and funds | Can capital and test funding be traced to legitimate sources? | Require Finance and MLRO certification with supporting records. |
| UBO/governance conflicts | Does any UBO also control an approval, vendor, custodian, or compliance function? | Require conflict declarations, recusal, and independent review. |
| Data protection | Will UBO identity data move across borders or through third parties? | Require DPO assessment, lawful basis, minimisation, security, and retention controls. |
| Freshness | Could ownership change between review and filing? | Require a no-change certificate and pre-submission re-screening. |
| Submission integrity | Can every UBO assertion be matched to a current document and hash? | Require an immutable evidence manifest and independent E-09 review. |

## Board-approved risk appetite for the proposed test

Subject to counsel and CBN agreement, the proposed risk appetite should be conservative: no customer-asset custody by Newwave, no token issuance, no unrestricted exchange or brokerage, no public retail access, no unapproved final settlement, hard participant and transaction limits, fail-closed screening and identity gates, immediate pause authority, complete reconciliation, documented consumer consent, and an approved wind-down plan.

These are proposed control positions, not evidence that the controls have already been approved or operated in a Nigerian live test.

## Board decision gates

The Board should use four formal gates. Gate 1 confirms the applicant entity and legal perimeter. Gate 2 confirms ownership, UBO, governance, financial capacity, and compliance evidence. Gate 3 confirms the controlled product/test perimeter and real staging evidence. Gate 4 authorises signature and filing only after independent review confirms no critical exception remains.

# 3. Mock CBN beneficial-ownership defense interview

This is a rehearsal, not a prediction of CBN questions. Participants should answer only from verified records. If an answer is unknown, the correct response is to identify the record owner and commit to producing it—not to speculate.

## Opening discipline for the applicant

> “We will distinguish verified corporate records, applicant-approved proposals, and matters still subject to counsel or CBN determination. We will not rely on public biographies as substitutes for ownership records, and we will provide the complete supporting documents through the secure channel.”

## Questions and model answer framework

| CBN question | Defensible model response | Evidence to have ready |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Which legal entity is applying? | “The applying entity is [exact legal name], incorporated in [jurisdiction], registration number [number]. It will [operate/contract/hold responsibility] for the proposed test. The relationship to NewWave Holdings and all affiliates is shown in the attached legal-entity map.” | Certificate, registry extract, Board resolution, entity map. |
| 2. What is the Nigerian connection? | “Our Nigerian connection is [entity/partner/representative/customer model]. Counsel has analysed the basis on which the proposed activity may be considered under the CBN VASP Track and has addressed any SEC or other regulatory interface.” | Local records, agreement/LOI, counsel opinion. |
| 3. Who are the UBOs? | “The UBO schedule identifies every natural person who ultimately owns or controls the applicant through shares, voting rights, contracts, appointment rights, trusts, nominees, or other arrangements. The schedule is reconciled to the ownership chart and supporting records.” | Signed UBO register, ownership chart, cap table, control-rights memo. |
| 4. How did you determine UBO status? | “We traced each corporate shareholder to natural persons and separately reviewed non-equity control. We documented ownership percentages, control bases, effective dates, and any conclusion where no person holds direct equity but a person exercises ultimate control.” | Ownership calculation, legal analysis, registry records, agreements. |
| 5. Is the founder automatically a UBO? | “No. A public title is not treated as proof of UBO status. We identify the person only if authoritative records show ownership or ultimate control. If the founder is a UBO, the basis and supporting records are stated; if not, the file explains why.” | Share register, control-rights records, counsel conclusion. |
| 6. What is the role of NewWave Holdings, LLC? | “The relationship is [parent/affiliate/other], effective from [date], and is supported by [records]. We have not treated the public website description as conclusive.” | Group chart, registry extracts, intercompany records. |
| 7. Are there nominee, trust, or shareholder-agreement arrangements? | “We have identified [none / the following arrangements]. For each, we have traced the nominator, settlor, trustee, protector, beneficiary, voting rights, and any person with appointment or veto rights.” | Trust/nominee documents, shareholder agreements, legal review. |
| 8. How do you verify UBO identity? | “Each UBO’s identity is matched to a valid identification record, issuing authority, issue and expiry information, and the UBO register. The records are stored in a restricted secure location and are not placed in Git or public materials.” | Identity package, access log, secure-store manifest. |
| 9. Have UBOs been screened? | “Yes, screening was performed on [date] using [approved methodology]. Results, false-positive rationale, PEP status, sanctions status, adverse media, enforcement history, and any EDD or escalation are recorded.” | Screening reports, methodology, EDD approvals. |
| 10. What if a UBO is a PEP? | “We will not minimise or conceal that fact. We will apply the approved enhanced due-diligence process, obtain the required senior approval, document source of wealth and funds, apply enhanced monitoring, and disclose the matter accurately.” | PEP assessment, EDD, senior approval, monitoring plan. |
| 11. What is the source of the applicant’s funding? | “The funding source is [verified source]. Finance and Compliance reconciled the funding flow from source to applicant and test account. Supporting records are available through the secure channel.” | Bank records, funding agreements, CFO/MLRO certifications. |
| 12. What prevents an undisclosed controller from influencing the test? | “The governance review covers voting agreements, appointment rights, veto rights, financing, related-party arrangements, and operational control. Material decisions require dual control and prohibited self-approval checks.” | Control-rights memo, authority matrix, audit logs. |
| 13. Does any UBO hold an approval or vendor role? | “We cross-reference UBOs against directors, officers, approvers, vendors, custodians, and partners. Conflicts are declared, recusals are recorded, and an independent reviewer approves the evidence.” | Conflict register, recusal records, role roster. |
| 14. How current is the UBO information? | “The register is dated [date], supported by current records, and accompanied by a signed no-change certificate. We re-screen before submission and before test entry.” | Registry search, no-change certificate, re-screen results. |
| 15. What happens if ownership changes after admission? | “The change triggers an internal escalation, reassessment, regulatory-notification decision, re-screening, and potential pause. We will follow the reporting and approval conditions agreed with CBN.” | Change-control policy, notification procedure, pause control. |
| 16. How is UBO personal data protected? | “Access is limited by role, data is minimised, transfers are assessed by the DPO, records are encrypted and retained only for the approved period, and immutable evidence contains hashes and references rather than unnecessary identity copies.” | PIA, access matrix, retention policy, audit logs. |
| 17. Are you using public records as your only evidence? | “No. Public sources support only the introductory company narrative. Ownership, UBO, financial, identity, and legal conclusions are based on authoritative records and independent review.” | Evidence register and source classification. |
| 18. What would cause you to pause the application? | “Any unresolved ownership conflict, unexplained controller, sanctions or PEP issue, source-of-funds concern, legal-perimeter uncertainty, inconsistent record, or inability to prove evidence integrity keeps the application NO-GO.” | Exception register, governance decision record. |
| 19. Does sandbox approval give you a licence? | “No. We will not represent sandbox admission as a licence. We will conduct only the activities and test conditions approved by CBN and any other relevant authority.” | Cover letter, marketing review, legal sign-off. |
| 20. What is your current status? | “The application is DRAFT / NO-GO. We are closing the entity, Nigerian nexus, UBO, financial, compliance, test-limit, and real staging-evidence requirements before signing or filing.” | Board minutes, blocker tracker, evidence status. |

## Red-flag answers to avoid

The applicant should not say “the founder is the owner” without records, “we have no UBO” without a documented investigation and legal conclusion, “our parent company handles it” without specifying the entity and control path, “the vendor performs screening so we are compliant” without oversight and evidence, “the sandbox is our licence,” or “we will provide the documents later” when the form requires them at submission.

## Interview evidence pack

The rehearsal team should have secure access to the entity map, current share register and cap table, UBO register, control-rights analysis, identity records, PEP/sanctions results, source-of-wealth/funds evidence, Board authority, governance role matrix, counsel opinion, test-boundary memo, financial records, and release-evidence manifest. The interviewer should record every question, answer, evidence reference, uncertainty, follow-up owner, and due date.
