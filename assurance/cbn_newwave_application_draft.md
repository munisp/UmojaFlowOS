## 7. Proposed VASP-track innovation

**Proposed title:** UmojaFlowOS — controlled, non-custodial VASP transaction-risk, governance, and evidence platform.

**Applicant-supplied proposal [VERIFY]:** UmojaFlowOS is intended to provide a controlled operating layer for VASP-related payment and digital-asset workflows, with strict product-boundary controls, AML/CFT/CPF screening integration, Travel Rule controls, dual-control approvals, immutable evidence capture, ledger reconciliation, incident response, and fail-closed retention controls.

**Problem statement:** VASPs and financial-infrastructure participants need a controlled way to test digital-asset and payment-related workflows while limiting transaction exposure, recording accountable decisions, screening risk, preserving evidence, and demonstrating operational resilience to regulators.

**Market benefit:** The proposed platform could reduce operational and compliance risk in controlled VASP testing by making transaction limits, approval separation, screening outcomes, reconciliation, incident records, and evidence integrity auditable. Any quantified market benefit must be supported by a market study or pilot data.

**Proposed in-scope capabilities [APPLICANT-SUPPLIED / VERIFY]:**

1. Non-custodial orchestration of tightly bounded test workflows.
2. AML/CFT/CPF and sanctions-screening integration through approved external providers.
3. Travel Rule data capture and message-control workflows.
4. Dual-control release approval and segregation-of-duties monitoring.
5. TigerBeetle-backed or equivalent double-entry ledger integration with reconciliation.
6. Keycloak-based identity, role enforcement, and auditable administrative access.
7. WORM/evidence-store publication with SHA-256 integrity checks and release binding.
8. Fail-closed incident, rollback, retention, and evidence-deletion controls.

**Explicit exclusions for the sandbox perimeter [APPLICANT-SUPPLIED / VERIFY]:**

1. No customer asset custody by Newwave unless separately approved and expressly included.
2. No token issuance or stablecoin issuance.
3. No unrestricted exchange, brokerage, or public retail trading.
4. No final settlement outside an authorised partner or approved test mechanism.
5. No use of customer funds beyond the approved transaction and volume limits.
6. No representation that sandbox admission is a licence.
7. No regulatory filing or approval action outside the official CBN channel.

The applicant must obtain legal advice on whether the proposed activity falls within CBN VASP-track scope, SEC jurisdiction, or another Nigerian regulatory perimeter. The public NewWave materials reviewed describe healthcare, IT, business services, data management, AI, cloud, and government-industry solutions; they do not publicly establish an existing Nigerian VASP operation.
