04 / Alerting & Notification

Critical Compliance Signals Route to Human Action

Critical Telemetry Alerts

Collector Availability (UmojaOTelCollectorDown)

Fires if the OTel Collector is unreachable for >2m. High compliance impact; settlement visibility lost.

Export Failures (UmojaOTelExportFailures)

Detects failing OTLP exports to backends. Prevents silent data loss in audit trails.

Refused Telemetry (UmojaComplianceTelemetryMissing)

Fires if the Collector refuses spans/metrics. Indicates buffer saturation or policy rejection.

Novu Notification Routing

1. Alertmanager Grouping: Alerts are grouped by tenant, service, and severity to prevent notification fatigue.
2. Inhibition Rules: Secondary warnings are suppressed if the primary Collector is down.
3. Novu Webhook Bridge: Critical signals are transformed into Novu events for multi-channel delivery.
4. Human Response: Responders receive direct links to runbooks and the compliance dashboard.
Note: All critical compliance alerts require an independent human sign-off for resolution in the audit log.
