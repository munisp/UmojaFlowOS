02 / Operating architecture
One observability path, two operational outcomes
The Collector centralizes telemetry policy; open-source backends separate alerting from trace investigation.

Services

Go, Rust, Python, TypeScript; bounded tenant context.

→

OTel Collector

OTLP intake, batching, memory limits, resource normalization.

→

Prometheus

Metrics scrape, recording rules, alert evaluation.

→

Alertmanager

Grouping, inhibition, routing, escalation.

→

Novu / Tempo

Human notification bridge and trace investigation.

Control outcome

Critical telemetry loss, ledger integrity, and compliance alerts remain visible and route to accountable responders.

Audit outcome

Trace and alert evidence can be retained, hashed, and bound to the tested release and staging run.
