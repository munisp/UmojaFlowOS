# Yellow Card Provider Research Notes

## Sources reviewed

1. https://yellowcard.io/api-suite — Yellow Card public API Suite page, accessed 2026-08-31. It describes stablecoin-powered cross-border payments, local-currency collections and payouts, global USD accounts, digital wallets, treasury solutions, and an API documentation link. The page shows a sandbox API base URL example but does not establish the exact contract, credentials, Nigerian product scope, or legal responsibility for a Newwave integration.

2. https://docs.yellowcard.engineering/ — Yellow Card public developer documentation, accessed 2026-08-31. It describes stablecoin payment infrastructure, fiat settlement rails, custody wallet services, and custom local stablecoin issuance as available product categories. It includes a public support contact and versioned documentation navigation. It does not establish that every product is available in Nigeria or approved for the proposed pilot.

3. https://yellowcard.io/legal/licenses-registration — Yellow Card public licenses and registrations page, updated August 4, 2026 and accessed 2026-08-31. It lists Yellow Card Financial Nigeria Limited, RC 1502209, as admitted to the SEC Accelerated Regulatory Incubation Programme as a Digital Assets Exchange on a conditional approval-in-principle basis and registered for financial-crime reporting with NFIU. This is a diligence lead only; the exact regulator record, conditions, contracting entity, product, corridor, and custody/settlement role must be verified directly.

4. https://sandbox.cbn.gov.ng/ — CBN Regulatory Sandbox portal, accessed 2026-08-31. Public content describes the VASP/stablecoin track, bounded live testing, customer onboarding, risk disclosure, complaints, cybersecurity, privacy, operational resilience, incident response, wind-down, and the fact that sandbox admission is not a permanent licence or authorization outside approved testing parameters.

5. https://sec.gov.ng/for-investors/keep-track-of-circulars/sec-clears-further-2-additional-vasps-for-admission-into-the-accelerated-regulatory-incubation-programme-arip/ — SEC Nigeria public ARIP material, accessed 2026-08-31. It states that Approval-in-Principle is conditional and is not a final licence. It reinforces the need to verify the exact regulated activity and entity rather than relying on a brand name.

## Use limitation

These notes support architecture and due-diligence planning only. They are not legal advice, regulator confirmation, an API contract, licensing evidence, or proof that a provider may perform the exact proposed transaction in Nigeria.
