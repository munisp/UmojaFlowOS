## 2. Ultimate beneficial ownership and control

NewWave will not infer UBOs from public leadership titles, social-media profiles, parent-company references, or third-party databases. It will trace the complete direct and indirect ownership and control chain of the applying entity to the relevant natural persons, including control exercised through shares, voting agreements, appointment rights, veto rights, financing, trusts, nominees, or other contractual arrangements.

The final application will include the required shareholder schedule, all UBO biographical information, identity evidence through the secure CBN submission process, ownership/control basis and effective dates, source-of-wealth and source-of-funds evidence, other-company interests, PEP declarations, sanctions and adverse-media screening, and documented treatment of any person who controls the applicant without holding equity.

NewWave will reconcile the UBO package against the current share register, cap table, corporate registry records, shareholder agreements, trust or nominee documentation, board records, and related-party registers. An independent reviewer who did not prepare the evidence will approve the reconciliation. Any unresolved conflict will keep the application in NO-GO status.

**Response status:** Open — critical blocker.  
**Closure evidence:** Signed ownership chart; current cap table and registry evidence; UBO register; identity records; control-rights analysis; source-of-wealth/funds package; PEP and sanctions results; independent reconciliation and legal sign-off.

## Critical path

The critical path is **entity decision → Nigerian regulatory opinion → ownership/UBO reconstruction → UBO screening and independent review → Board/signatory authority → financial evidence → product/test perimeter → AML/CFT/CPF and privacy controls → staging evidence → E-09 → final audit**. Delays in the entity decision or ownership package will delay every downstream approval.

## Weekly governance cadence

The programme sponsor should hold a weekly blocker review with Legal, MLRO, Finance, Product Risk, Security, DPO, Operations, and the evidence custodian. Each meeting should record the status of every checklist item, evidence owner, reviewer, due date, dependency, residual risk, and decision. A critical blocker cannot be closed by narrative assurance alone; it requires the specified evidence and an accountable approval.

## GO decision rule

Transition to **READY FOR SUBMISSION** only when all critical items are closed, all evidence is real and current, all UBO and regulatory declarations are reconciled, all required approvals are independent, and the authorised signatory certifies completeness. Transition to **GO FOR CONTROLLED TESTING** only after CBN approval and agreed test conditions; submission readiness is not permission to commence live activity.
