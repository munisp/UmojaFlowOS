## Direct documentation verification

The reviewed Circle Mint overview identifies Circle Mint as an institutional product, states that customers deposit fiat from a linked bank account, and documents conversion to USDC/EURC and blockchain transfer flows. It therefore cannot be treated as a general-purpose retail conversion or as proof of an enabled NGN/KES/ZAR product. The reviewed Fireblocks API overview describes a REST API, a public OpenAPI specification, API/JWT authentication guidance, sandbox tooling, vault/fiat-account/network/transaction objects, policy/editor objects, and webhooks. It does not prove the existence of a Fireblocks workspace, policy approval, connected account, custody arrangement, balance, or settlement authority for UmojaFlowOS.
