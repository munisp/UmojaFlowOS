# CBN Regulatory Sandbox Cohort 2 — Verified Source Notes

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-19 (EDT)  
**Primary source:** `https://sandbox.cbn.gov.ng/`

The official Central Bank of Nigeria Regulatory Sandbox website presents **Cohort 2** as open and links the Cohort 2 call for application, the Regulatory Sandbox Framework, and a Financial Service Provider user guide. It presents two testing tracks: a **Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) Track** and a **Data-Enabled Financial Services (Non-VASP) Track**.

The page describes the VASP Track as covering stablecoins, payment tokens, security digital assets that perform a payment, settlement, or store-of-value function, and related payments/data-enabled innovations. Its Cohort 2 materials state that supervised testing is subject to defined testing parameters and safeguards for consumer protection, operational resilience, cybersecurity, and regulatory reporting.

The official page expressly states that sandbox admission does **not** amount to a permanent licence or regulatory approval to operate beyond the approved testing parameters. It also states that any post-sandbox pathway remains subject to applicable legal, regulatory, prudential, conduct, and supervisory requirements.

The uploaded notice gives the application window as 12–31 August 2026. The official page was live on 19 August 2026 and corroborates the programme, tracks, and controlled-testing positioning. Date-specific application status must be confirmed at submission time from the official portal rather than inferred from this note.

## Requirement extraction for the platform assessment

The official Cohort 2 page requires every applicant to demonstrate a live-test-ready product, assessed and mitigated inherent risks, effective AML/CFT/CPF controls, appropriate consumer safeguards, and the technical ability to comply with reporting and incident-notification requirements during the sandbox period. Its readiness guidance further names a legal entity; disclosed shareholders and beneficial owners; a working MVP or more advanced product; a testing plan with customer segments, expected volumes, boundaries, and success measures; designated management, compliance/MLRO, risk, technology, and cybersecurity personnel; corporate approvals; and governance, financial, technical, risk, consumer-protection, and data-privacy documentation.

The testing safeguards expressly include defined limits on user categories, transaction volumes, customer exposure, and test duration; customer onboarding; risk disclosures; complaints handling; cybersecurity; data protection and privacy; operational resilience; business continuity; incident response; and orderly wind-down. Material cyber, fraud, or consumer-harm incidents must be reported promptly under the sandbox requirements.

For VASP or stablecoin propositions, the page calls for relevant governance, technology, reserves, disclosure, and risk-control documentation. Its FAQs name reserve-management arrangements, reserve attestations, redemption mechanisms, liquidity-management policies, stablecoin governance documentation, customer-fund/digital-asset safeguarding and segregation, custody/wallet architecture, key management, hot/warm/cold-wallet allocation, and asset-segregation controls. It also identifies AML/CFT policies and risk assessment, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, training, and customer due diligence procedures as documents that may be requested.
